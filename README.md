# 6100 Partners Launch

Build a modern, high-end corporate landing page for 6100 Partners, LLC, an executive advisory and operational leadership firm founded by Brandon Oliver, MBA.

Positioning & Key Areas:
- Healthcare Services & HealthTech Operations & Scaling
- Private Equity Value Creation, Portfolio Operations, & Operational Diligence
- Fractional / Interim COO & Executive Advisory
- Operational Turnarounds, Transformation, & P&L Optimization
- Multi-State Operations & Value-Based Healthcare Models

Sections & Features:
1. Hero Section: Refined, institutional aesthetic (deep navy/slate/dark charcoal palette with subtle bronze/gold accents, crisp typography), strong value proposition, and primary 'Request Consultation' CTA.
2. Value Proposition / Strategic Overview: How 6100 Partners partners with private equity sponsors, boards, and founders to accelerate enterprise value and scale complex operations.
3. Core Practice Areas / Services:
   - Private Equity Value Creation & Portfolio Support
   - Fractional / Interim Operational Leadership (COO)
   - Healthcare Services & HealthTech Growth
   - Turnaround & Operating Efficiency
4. Leadership Profile: Founder & Executive Advisor Brandon Oliver, MBA, highlighting 20+ years across healthcare services, technology, and PE-backed operating leadership.
5. Operating Pillars & Impact: Highlights of proven methodologies (Scalable Systems, P&L Rigor, Execution speed).
6. Contact & Engagement Form: Interactive consultation request form with clear inquiry routing and scheduling link.
7. Polished footer with Nashville/national reach and professional copyright.
